FAKE CHURCH, 2026

Project

A fictional church for the age when people hand their judgment over to algorithms.

FAKE CHURCH builds the parts of a religion for a god made of data: scripture, clergy, an altar, and the clothes its followers wear. Its altar is the installation Dataism, which inscribes AI activity from around the world onto a cross of LEDs in real time.
