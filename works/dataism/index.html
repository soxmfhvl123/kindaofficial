Dataism, 2026

FAKE CHURCH

An installation that receives AI activity from around the world in real time and inscribes it on a cross of LEDs.

It starts from an observation: more and more of our judgment is handed to machines, and the line between what we decide and what is decided for us grows faint. Dataism makes that handover visible as worship.

Twelve live feeds run across sixteen LED panels: which AI services are up or down, how many commits were written with AI in the last minute, which models were released today, what people are saying about them. Nothing is pre-recorded. It is the altar of FAKE CHURCH.

Live — the software that runs on the cross, running in your browser now.
